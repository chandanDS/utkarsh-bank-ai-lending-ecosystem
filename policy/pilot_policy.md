# Illustrative pilot policy — NOT Bajaj Finserv policy

This document is a placeholder for an approved, versioned Bajaj Finserv policy. Replace it with authorized source material before any real decision or customer contact. Every decision must record the policy version and supporting passage.

## Pilot population and consent
Only customers in the assigned pilot cohort may be processed. Apply contact consent, channel preference, do-not-contact, quiet hours, and frequency caps before a campaign. A control cohort receives the existing journey so incremental performance can be measured.

## Eligibility gates
For demonstration only: applicant age 21–65, monthly income at least ₹20,000, bureau score at least 650, no active severe delinquency, and verified identity. A failed gate leads to review or decline with a reason code. Missing bureau or account aggregator data should trigger review, not an automatic decline.

## Offer and risk
For demonstration only: projected EMI should stay within 45% of verified monthly income after current EMI. Maximum term is 36 months. Higher risk receives a lower eligible amount or manual review. Pricing must follow an approved rate card and applicable regulatory rules; model output alone cannot set a binding rate.

## Pre-approved and pre-qualified
Pre-approved requires current verified identity, high data completeness, low risk, and a valid approved offer. Pre-qualified means indicative eligibility subject to full underwriting, verification, and final approval. Customer-facing wording must distinguish these states.

## Underwriting and disbursal
Final approval needs KYC, fraud checks, affordability, bureau and account aggregator review where consented, policy checks, agreement acceptance, and authorized disbursal controls. High impact decisions and exceptions are reviewable by a human credit officer. Log input snapshots, reason codes, model and policy versions, overrides, and consent.

## Collections
Use proportionate, consent-aware reminders and self-service payment options. Never use sensitive characteristics or abusive contact practices. Prioritize early help, payment plans where approved, and escalation to trained staff for hardship or disputes.
